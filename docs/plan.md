## Principles

1. Platform is the source of truth. Connor's `platform/web` and `libs/grounded-sdk` (main 8ab3dad, #146-#244) decide the data, the rules, the flows and the words. demo-product follows them wherever a demo can without a real backend.
2. Delete ruthlessly. When platform makes something redundant, or platform doesn't have it and the demo doesn't need it, remove the code, its tests, its Walkthrough beats and its doc lines in the same change. No second version, no dead flags, no "just in case" code. Leave every folder smaller.
3. Keep the flavor. The product keeps Krish's look and tone: tokens, type, spacing, tags and chips, one tab style, calm copy, one primary action per view, the stepper. Platform decides what a screen does; demo-product decides how it looks and reads.
4. Track everything. Every behavior change, removal and copy change goes into [changes.md](changes.md) (by area) so Krish can review it and update his Notion Product experience. Decisions are at the end of this doc.
5. Stay demo-able. Walkthroughs, Get started, Reset demo, the in-memory backend and recorded Pebble answers stay; the Unity Demo call keeps working at the end of each phase.

Krish's exceptions to following platform: the stage stepper stays, his Candidate branching (save and compare) is built, and his Oct 14 meeting notes are built in Phase 2. ⌘K and Pebble @mentions stay.

## Architecture now

- `src/sdk` is platform's `libs/grounded-sdk/src` byte for byte (`check:platform` pins it), its `index.ts` included
- `src/platform` is platform's `web/src` at its own paths: the router and routes, `queries.ts`, the shell, sidebar, Pebble and the `ui` kit. `scripts/platform-web.json` pins it and gives each file a kind (copy, patch, shim, fork, skip) with a reason for every patch, shim and fork; `check:platform` checks it and `sync:platform` moves it ([README](../README.md) has the details)
- Our look and additions live outside the mirror (`src/features`, `src/ui`, `src/styles`, `src/queries/extra.ts`, `src/app`), called from small patches or rendered by forks where ours reads clearer (the keep list above)
- Pebble's chat is platform's Pi session over the thread socket (`src/platform/pebble/session.ts` and `threads.ts`, patched); the forks of `chat.tsx`, `conversation.tsx`, `composer.tsx` and `thread-picker.tsx` render ours from `src/features/pebble`. In the demo, `demo/plane/thread-socket.ts` serves that socket in the page from the viewer's layer, recordings and the Worker
- Screens call one `ProductClient` (`src/queries/client.ts`): platform's `GroundedClient` resources plus Pebble's threads. Against a real Org that is `new GroundedClient(...)`
- In the demo, `src/demo/plane` serves in-memory `repos`, `git`, `pulls`, `agents` and `jobs` and runs platform's own `Issues` and `Evals` over them, so Issue rules, Candidate results, approve and ship are platform's code
- demo-data writes agent repos platform could have created (template layout, frontmatter, registry, trailers), and its Traces and annotations as the OTLP/HTTP JSON bodies platform's collector takes (`traces/<xx>.json` on `/v1/traces`, `logs/<run>.json` on `/v1/logs`), which the backend decodes the way the collector and agents service read them; stored runs' Eval trials are Traces of their own, and only a run played during the demo replays a recording (`recordings/`) at read time

- Errors in 3 layers: the raw Trace, the step it failed at, and the Grade's reasoning
- Pebble hints on failing Grades, runs and trials; empty selections explain and disable
- Flow view of a session: lanes per message type, folded runs, failing steps with their reasoning and inline step detail

### 2D. Pebble and panes (Krish, Danny, Oct 14 evening, done)

- Pebble toggles from the side whenever you want (kept).
- Click anywhere in the side chat to expand it into the main view; navigating to another page collapses it back to the side, carrying where you are as context.
- One conversation everywhere: the main view and the side panel show the same thread, live.
- The side panel gets rounded corners and slim margins.
- Debug can go full screen.
- Panes like Cursor or Arc: swap which pane is primary (files or Pebble), split side by side, and drag a pane to another side.
- Built as a small model, not a docking framework: Pebble's side in a saved store (`app/panes.ts`), the widths in the panel layout, full screen in the shell, the main view as platform's chat routes (`/chat/<thread>`, since #215), and one mounted chat moved between the views through a portal (platform's #216), so both views show one chat. Pane chrome is `ui/pane.tsx` (layout menu, drag and drop zones)
